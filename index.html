Lima

Lima is the capital of Peru and the country's largest city, located along the Pacific coast. It is known for its rich history, vibrant neighborhoods, and a unique blend of colonial and modern architecture that reflects its cultural heritage.

The city is also recognized as one of South America's top culinary destinations, offering world-famous cuisine, beautiful ocean views, and a thriving arts scene. As Peru's economic and cultural center, Lima continues to attract visitors from around the world.
